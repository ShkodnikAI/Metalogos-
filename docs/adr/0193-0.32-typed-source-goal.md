## §1. Context — the S-3 lesson (the goal that executes itself)

The 0.31 sensitive-surface goal (ADR-0191, the variant «а», 4782 bp)
was REACHED — by the denominator growth. The audit's arithmetic: the
covered share moved 66/138 → 73/145 (4782 → 5034 bp) while the
untyped set did not move — `untyped 67` at the fixation anchor AND at
the audit window's end. The +7 covered sources were the NEW PE
surfaces (HTML/XML parsing, Telegram, headers) typed at birth — every
OLD uncovered source stayed uncovered. A goal that a project satisfies
by the mere act of writing it down (the X-3 picture) does not move
anything; the metric ratchet (the only-up floor) is honest, but the
GOAL FORM was exploitable by construction.

The root cause: the goal was expressed over a SHARE — the denominator
(the source count) is free to grow, so the share can be «improved»
without touching the numerator's honest substance (which sources are
covered).

## §2. The proposed decision — the FIXED-LIST closure form

The 0.32 goal is expressed over a FIXED list, never over a share:

> **Of the 67 untyped sensitive sources recorded at the anchor
> `f92c61c` (v0.30.1, 2026-10-14), at least 20 are CLOSED by the 0.32
> release — the ПДн-class rows first. New sources do not count: they
> are typed at birth (the №687 surface rule keeps them on naryads).**

The mechanics, fixed by this draft:

1. **The list is machine-readable and checked in**:
   `scripts/ci/gate_032_untyped_sources.txt` — 67 rows of
   `id registry-file:line-at-anchor`, generated by the anchor
   worktree's own №644 inventory (the S-3 table reproduced exactly:
   67 untyped at `f92c61c`). The list is FROZEN as a fact of the
   anchor: ids are never added (new sources are typed at birth — the
   goal does not reward adding untyped rows first), and never removed
   (a closed id stays in the list as closed — the closure is counted
   once, by the id).
2. **The closure definition reuses the №644 mirrors verbatim** (no
   second parser): an id is CLOSED when the row leaves the uncovered
   set — typed ∧ (scalar ∨ parameterized) ∧ (¬Struct ∨ all fields
   labeled). The counter: `sensitive_surface_share.py --goal032
   scripts/ci/gate_032_untyped_sources.txt` (the report-only mode,
   GREEN at the start — the honest 0/20 posture) and `--gate032
   <list> [figure]` (the floor mode, exit 1 below the figure).
3. **The denominator is OUT of the goal.** The share and the bp floor
   (the №644 ratchet) keep existing as the regression guard, but the
   0.32 goal reads ONLY the fixed-list closures.

## §5. Honest limits — what INSTITUTIONAL_READY is NOT

- **Not legal force.** The machine verifies the PRESENCE and STRUCTURE
  of the policies and their integrity (the cross-references, the
  append-only journal) — not their legal power. A filed trademark, an
  incorporated structure, a bank account are the OWNER'S ACTS (named
  per R38(9): the trademark filing, the neutral-structure
  incorporation, the banking, the partner contacts) — outside the
  repository, outside the naryads, outside this machine's claims.
- **Not legal advice.** The frame is the C10 verbatim: «конфигурация
  ограничений, не юридический совет». The documents describe the
  project's own contours; they do not counsel any external party.
- **Not a succession execution.** The activation journal (item 9) is
  EMPTY by design — no activation has occurred; the document prepares
  the path, it does not walk it. The second maintainer (№774,
  verified-wait, gh#1437) has not joined — the authority chain
  references the single-holder reality (the owner as the sole code
  owner in .github/CODEOWNERS).
- **Not the renaming.** The №503 rename (Metalogos- → Metalogos) is an
  owner act, strictly last, outside this wave.
